Our Story

From Erode to Across India

Founded in 2010, Siva Suresh Agency started as a small textile supply unit in Erode, Tamil Nadu — a city known for its rich heritage in textile manufacturing.

Over the past 15+ years, we have grown into one of the most trusted suppliers of hospital linen, medical uniforms, and hotel textiles, serving 500+ hospitals and institutions across India.

ISO Quality Standards

Every product meets strict quality benchmarks before dispatch.

100% Cotton Fabrics

Premium combed cotton for lasting comfort and durability.

Pan-India Delivery

Reliable shipping to all states with tracked shipments.

Custom Manufacturing

Logos, embroidery, custom sizes & colors on demand.

Our Journey

15+ Years of Growth & Excellence

2010 — Founded

The Beginning

Siva Suresh Agency was established in Erode, Tamil Nadu with a small team and a big vision to supply premium hospital textiles.

2013 — Expansion

First 100 Hospital Partnerships

Reached the milestone of supplying to 100+ hospitals across Tamil Nadu, building a reputation for quality and reliability.

2017 — Innovation

CliniFlex™ Brand Launched

Introduced our signature scrub suit line — CliniFlex™ — combining premium fabric with modern medical fashion.

2020 — Pan-India

All-India Delivery Network

Expanded logistics to all Indian states, offering free shipping on bulk orders and express delivery options.

2023 — Digital

Online Platform Launch

Launched our full e-commerce platform enabling online ordering, bulk procurement, and real-time order tracking.

2025 — Today

500+ Hospitals, 1000+ Happy Clients

Serving 500+ hospitals and institutions across India with a catalogue of 50+ products and growing.
