One tree runs the whole site. Build your category structure in three levels:

  • Main Heading — Top-level menu column (e.g. "Doctor Uniform")
  • Main Category — Underneath headings (e.g. "Male Doctor Uniform")
  • Sub Category — Under main categories (e.g. "Full Sleeve")

? What this controls: Header dropdown menu, shop filters, and where products are organized. Products are filed against a Main Category (and optionally a Sub Category).

? Inline editing: Rename anything inline. Menu thumbnails are pulled automatically from your product images.

? Signature Collections: Mark a heading as "Signature" to show it as a premium highlighted collection (like CliniFlex™ Scrubs)

? Reordering: Drag by the icon to reorder. Changes sync everywhere on the frontend in ~2 min

? Publish: Click "Save & Publish" to push changes live
